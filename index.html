Python Patrol

In this project I practiced several of of the skills learnt on my web localization course.

Competencies Self evaluation

This project is a translation of Alaina Brandt's page on the professional competencies a translator has for their translation skills, their language skills, their networking skills and their managing skills. In this project, I practiced translating different linked pages and changing styles to better match an audience.

View Project

Terminology and AI

This project is a translation of Alaina Brandt's page on the challenges and problems of the overuse of AI for translation and terminology. It cover the essential concepts to understand the process humans use for terminology and the way it differs from the processes used by LLMs. With this project I practiced the correct usage of terminology and the managing and localizing of different parts of a web page.
